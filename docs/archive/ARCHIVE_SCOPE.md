# Archive scope

This directory preserves the idea-development record that led to WAM2Repair: early WAM/VLA landscape maps, candidate-method reviews, research contracts, literature notes, and the focused WAM-prediction-error-correction novelty investigation.

It is historical context, not experimental evidence. In particular, archived candidate ideas and synthetic/toy diagnostics must not be presented as validated WAM, geometry-repair, action-ranking, or pi0.5 closed-loop results. The current method and evidence boundary are defined by the top-level `docs/` and `docs/reports/` documents.

Machine identities, absolute paths, and server-specific instructions have been replaced with placeholders. Raw chat handoffs, credentials, model weights, datasets, checkpoints, and generated rollouts are intentionally absent.
