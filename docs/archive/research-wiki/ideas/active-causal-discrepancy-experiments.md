# Active Causal Discrepancy Experiments

**stage:** `proposed`  ·  **outcome:** `pending`

Use information-gain diagnostic actions to identify execution, WAM, or observation causes before source-specific memory and recovery updates.

## Thesis
Residual-matched causal hypotheses become actionable only when a safe intervention makes them identifiable and changes the optimal memory/WAM/recovery response.

## Key risks
Short RGB/proprioceptive probes may not identify causes; without active intervention the method collapses to a low-novelty router.
