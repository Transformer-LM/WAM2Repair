# Witness-Carrying Progress Actions

**stage:** `proposed`  ·  **outcome:** `pending`

Tune ordinary next-task actions to jointly make progress and reveal whether a pending task predicate is true.

## Thesis
A progress-positive action can also serve as a physical witness, reducing false memory commits without a separate verifier gate.

## Key risks
Informative progress-positive actions may be rare; strong overlap with active perception, dual control, ProgressVLA, and FlowPRO.
